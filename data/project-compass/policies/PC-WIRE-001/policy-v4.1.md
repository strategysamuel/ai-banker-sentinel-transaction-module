## 1. Purpose
Provide procedural guidelines for executing outbound international telegraphic transfers (SWIFT/Wires) under the legacy 2025 risk framework.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all retail and commercial branch staff processing customer-initiated cross-border funds transfers.

## 3. Definitions
- **DEMO_LIMIT_A**: Historical synthetic threshold of $5,000.00 USD equivalent requiring branch manager verbal callback.

## 4. Standard Procedures & Controls
### Section 1.0: Wire Order Capture (Legacy Protocol)
- **Citation ID:** `PC-WIRE-001-v4.1-section-1`
- **Source Reference:** `demo://project-compass/PC-WIRE-001/v4.1/section/1.0`
- **Applicable Roles:** Branch Banker

Banker captures beneficiary SWIFT BIC, IBAN, beneficiary name, physical address, and remittance purpose. Banker verifies physical signature against signature card.



### Section 2.0: Authorization Thresholds (Legacy Protocol)
- **Citation ID:** `PC-WIRE-001-v4.1-section-2`
- **Source Reference:** `demo://project-compass/PC-WIRE-001/v4.1/section/2.0`
- **Applicable Roles:** Branch Banker, Senior Branch Banker

Supervisor approval and verbal callback verification are required for all transactions exceeding DEMO_LIMIT_A ($5,000 USD equivalent). Transactions below DEMO_LIMIT_A may be released with standard branch banker single authorization.




## 5. Exceptions
- Governmental remittance exceptions allowed with senior operations sign-off.

## 6. Approval Requirements & Thresholds
- Supervisor review required above DEMO_LIMIT_A ($5,000 USD).

## 7. Escalation Rules
- Escalate to Wire Operations Desk if intermediary bank routing is missing.
